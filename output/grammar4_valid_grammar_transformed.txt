
--- ORIGINAL GRAMMAR ---
Prog -> Stmt Prog | epsilon
Stmt -> id assign Expr | print Expr
Expr -> Expr plus Term | Term
Term -> num | id

--- AFTER LEFT FACTORING ---
Prog -> Stmt Prog | epsilon
Stmt -> id assign Expr | print Expr
Expr -> Expr plus Term | Term
Term -> num | id

--- AFTER LEFT RECURSION REMOVAL ---
Prog -> Stmt Prog | epsilon
Stmt -> id assign Expr | print Expr
Expr -> Term Expr'
Expr' -> plus Term Expr' | epsilon
Term -> num | id
